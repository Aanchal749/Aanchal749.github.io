WalkSafe AI

Done at e-Yantra, IIT Bombay with the Walking Project NGO. The goal is to show people what a footpath looks like before and after it is fixed.

  • Matrix-Game 3.0
  • LoRA
  • SegFormer
  • YOLOv8
  • FastAPI

What I built

  • Compared 12 world models, kept the 6 that are truly interactive, and picked Matrix-Game 3.0.
  • Ran it on an NVIDIA DGX Spark server over SSH.
  • Wrote footpath action logic using SegFormer and YOLOv8, and fixed inside out rendering with a camera direction check.
  • Fine tuned a DiT with LoRA for 500 epochs on 45 real road videos. Best loss was 0.3588 at epoch 142.
  • Built the WalkSafe AI website with a FastAPI backend and side by side before and after videos.

Also

  • Wan 2.1, Yume, LingBot-World and Cosmos 3 did not run on the server's ARM (aarch64) setup.
  • Also built a pedestrian compliance auditor with YOLOv8 and ByteTrack.
