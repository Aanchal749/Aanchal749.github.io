AgriYukt

AgriYukt connects farmers to APMC wholesale buyers. Many farmers have no smartphone habits or no signal, so field inspectors onboard them and list crops on their behalf.

  • Flutter
  • Node.js
  • Supabase
  • JWT and RBAC

Top 16 at the AIC Eata Innovation Challenge

What I built

  • Flutter mobile app, live on the Google Play Store.
  • Node.js backend with 9 modules: auth, user, crops, orders, wallet, notifications, inspector, bank and market.
  • Supabase Auth with JWT roles for farmers and inspectors, row level security policies, and crop image storage.
  • Explored phone OTP, offline Aadhaar XML, and GSTIN and PAN checks for business buyers.

Also

  • Visited APMC markets to understand how buyers actually trade.
  • Compared Ninjacart, DeHaat, Arya.ag and AgroStar.
  • Started as SurveyGov, a GIS survey tool for Maharashtra government field officers using Google Earth Engine NDVI.
